Who We Are

Your Local Card Shop

Wax, Packs, & Throwbacks is Atlantic County's destination for sports cards, Pokémon & TCG, trading cards, sports memorabilia, and vintage collectibles. Whether you're a seasoned collector chasing a grail or a newcomer cracking your first pack, we've got something for everyone.

We carry the newest releases from Topps, Panini, and more alongside a deep inventory of vintage wax packs, graded cards, and throwback memorabilia. Stop in, say hi, and let's talk cards.

Sports Cards
Baseball, basketball, football & hockey — new releases and vintage singles
Pokémon & TCG
Sealed product, singles, booster boxes & tournament play
Wax Pack Breaks

Graded Cards
