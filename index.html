Who We Are

Your Local Card Shop

Wax Packs & Throwbacks is Linwood, NJ's destination for sports cards, trading cards, and vintage collectibles. Whether you're a seasoned collector chasing a grail or a newcomer cracking your first pack, we've got something for everyone.

We carry the newest releases from Topps, Panini, and more alongside a deep inventory of vintage wax packs, graded cards, and throwback memorabilia. Stop in, say hi, and let's talk cards.
